Sure. Let me explain it very simply.

What you are thinking

A person in Chennai wants to send his car to Tirunelveli.

Normally:

Car transport company charges around ₹15,000–₹20,000.


Your app:

Finds a traveler already going to Tirunelveli.

Traveler takes the car, travels with his family, and drops it.

Traveler pays fuel and toll.

Owner gets his car delivered cheaper.


This idea is good.


---

The problem

Both people think they are helping the other person.

Owner says:

> "I am giving my car. Why should I pay much?"



Traveler says:

> "I am driving your car and delivering it. Why should I pay much?"



So if you charge too much to either side, they won't use the app.


---

Simple example

Normal transport company:

₹18,000


Your app:

Owner pays:

₹8,000


Traveler pays:

Fuel + Toll = ₹5,000

App fee = ₹2,000


Total traveler cost:

₹7,000


Result:

✅ Owner saves ₹10,000

✅ Traveler gets a car trip for ₹7,000

✅ App earns ₹10,000 (₹8,000 + ₹2,000) before expenses

Everybody wins.


---

What I would avoid

Don't say:

> BMW = ₹5,000 extra



> Audi = ₹8,000 extra



Initially, keep it simple.

Instead:

Normal Cars

Swift

i20

Baleno

Amaze


Premium Cars

BMW

Mercedes

Fortuner


Only 2 categories.

People understand this easily.


---

The real attraction

The traveler is not paying to "shift a car."

The traveler is paying for:

> "I can take my family on a road trip in a car without buying or renting one."



That is the emotional benefit.

For example:

A family of 4 wants to go from Chennai to Ooty.

Instead of:

Bus

Train

Expensive rental car


They use your app.

They get:

A private car

Family trip

Luggage space

Freedom to stop anywhere


That is what they are really buying.


---

My final suggestion

Keep it very simple:

Owner

Pays a reduced relocation fee.


Traveler

Pays fuel + toll + a small usage fee.


App

Provides verification, insurance, tracking, and support.


Don't add too many complicated charges at the beginning.

The simpler the pricing, the more likely people are to trust and use the service.
